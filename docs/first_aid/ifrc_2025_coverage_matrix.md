| Asthma | Replaced regional 4x4x4 blue/grey protocol with IFRC 2025 bronchodilator/spacer/action-plan guidance | `asthma-attack` |
| Anemia | Not an IFRC 2025 First Aid emergency chapter; replaced by underlying acute emergency presentations | `shock`, `severe-bleeding`, `feeling-faint`, `breathing-difficulties` |
| Nosebleed | Standardized to IFRC 2025 >=10 min continuous soft-nose pinch and >20 min medical escalation | `nosebleeds` |
| Hyperventilation | Subsumed in IFRC 2025 under Breathing Difficulties & Anxiety/Panic (with explicit no-paper-bag warning); retained via search synonym | `anxiety-and-panic`, `breathing-difficulties` |
| Moving an injured person | Replaced generic wheelchair/stretcher text with IFRC 2025 Scene Safety & Spinal Injury immobilization rules; retained via search synonym | `scene-safety`, `spinal-injury` |
| Basic life support (BLS) / CPR | Separated into age-specific CPR + dedicated AED procedure per IFRC 2025 | `cpr-adult`, `cpr-child`, `cpr-infant`, `aed-use` |
| Choking (blocked airway) | Separated into age-specific Adult, Child, and Infant Choking procedures per IFRC 2025 | `choking-adult`, `choking-child`, `choking-infant` |
| Bleeding and wound care | Removed routine limb elevation; separated into Severe Bleeding, Cuts & Grazes, Amputation, and Chest/Abdomen Injuries | `severe-bleeding`, `cuts-and-grazes`, `amputation`, `chest-abdomen-injuries` |

| Fractures, sprains, dislocations & seizures | Unbundled unrelated musculoskeletal trauma and neurological emergencies; removed routine compression bandaging | `fractures`, `sprains`, `strains`, `spinal-injury`, `seizures` |
